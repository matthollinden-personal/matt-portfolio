## Featured case study: Mobile Parking Lot

**Context:** GE Appliances · Salesforce delivery and installation system implementation.

**Role:** Principal Program Manager · discovery through launch.

Most orders are delivered to customers, but some customers collect their orders at the warehouse—a process known as Will Call. As part of the broader delivery and installation system implementation in Salesforce, I led Mobile Parking Lot, a workflow that brings mobile inventory scanning to these customer pickups.

### The problem

The previous system only allowed users to bill for everything at once. It lacked a mobile way to scan inventory out of the warehouse and verify what was leaving, creating a gap in inventory and billing accuracy.

### My contribution

I led the process from start to finish: working with end users to understand their needs, writing acceptance criteria, supporting solution design, testing, training, and launching the workflow.

### The outcome

The workflow improved end-user confidence in the pickup process. Mobile scanning is also expected to help prevent future inventory discrepancies; that benefit has not been quantified.

Results are qualitative. No quantitative data is shared, and the anticipated reduction in inventory discrepancies is not presented as a measured result.
